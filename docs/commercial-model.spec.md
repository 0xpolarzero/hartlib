# Commercial Model

## Current product boundary

The shipped product is a client research demo over authorized public-source
documents. It has no billing, payment, export, notification, account, or
support route. The active session, chat, memory, source, and AI contracts are
the only customer-data paths.

## Future model

Hartlib may later charge companies for AI usage and may later support paid
publication workflows. Those decisions are deferred. No price, plan, credit,
invoice, or payment claim is part of the current runtime, API, database
migration, or production bundle.

The current worker records provider usage and web-operation counts as run data.
This is operational evidence, not a billable balance. Any future billing
design must define its own account, authorization, idempotency, retention, and
disclosure contract before adding a route or table.

## Trust promises

The demo sends only accepted, authorized context to the configured provider.
Prompts, answers, memories, source text, and provider bodies remain restricted
run data. Public projections show safe status, counts, citations, and document
targets only. Production provider, region, retention, training, and pricing
claims stay deferred until the production-readiness evidence workflow accepts
them.
